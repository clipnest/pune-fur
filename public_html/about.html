About us

Made in Kothrud. Sold from the same door.

Who we are

One address. Workshop and showroom.

We make furniture in our own workshop and show it in our own showroom, both at Banadal Capital on Paud Road, Kothrud. You can see where your furniture is made, and you can sit on it before you order.

We serve home buyers and businesses across Pune, including West Pune, and PCMC. Delivery is free across Pune and PCMC.

Our aim is simple: furniture that fits your room, built at an honest price, with a reply on WhatsApp and a quote you can compare.
