FAQs

Custom Mattress & Gaddi FAQs

Can you make a mattress for an odd-sized or vintage wooden bed?

Yes! That is our specialty. Measure the inner lip of your wooden bed frame (length and width in inches or centimeters), and we will manufacture a mattress that fits snugly without sliding.

What is the difference between sofa gaddi and bed mattresses?

Sofa gaddi requires higher density foam (32D to 40D HR foam) with resilient bounce back to handle concentrated point pressure without bottoming out. Bed mattresses use layered comfort zones for distributed body weight.

Do you provide removable and washable covers for sofa gaddi?

Yes, all custom sofa gaddi and cushions include heavy-duty concealed zippers so covers can be easily removed for machine washing or dry cleaning.
